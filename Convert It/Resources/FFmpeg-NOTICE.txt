ConvertIt — FFmpeg third-party notice
=====================================

This software uses libraries from the FFmpeg project under the LGPLv2.1.

ConvertIt invokes FFmpeg as a separate command-line process for certain video
and audio conversions. It does not link FFmpeg libraries into the ConvertIt
application binary.

Bundled FFmpeg builds are compiled without --enable-gpl and without
--enable-nonfree. H.264 output uses the h264_videotoolbox encoder (Apple
VideoToolbox), not GPL libx264.
